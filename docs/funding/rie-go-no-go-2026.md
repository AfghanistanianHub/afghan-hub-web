# PacifiCan RIE — Afghan Hub Go / No-Go Assessment

**Verified:** 2026-10-01  
**Program:** Regional Innovation Ecosystems (RIE), British Columbia  
**Deadline:** October 30, 2026 at 12:00 p.m. PT  
**Funding:** Non-repayable contributions, normally not exceeding $3M, with 50% matching funding.

## What RIE is actually for

RIE supports business growth in British Columbia by funding organizations that help businesses, innovators and entrepreneurs improve productivity, commercialize, scale, increase revenues/exports and create high-quality jobs.

The program is not primarily a community-inclusion or settlement grant.

## Applicant fit

Typical eligible applicants include:

- industry associations;
- municipalities;
- post-secondary institutions;
- Indigenous organizations/businesses;
- social enterprises;
- angel networks;
- business accelerators and ecosystem organizations;
- groups/consortia of eligible recipients.

All applicants must be legal entities capable of entering into a binding agreement.

For-profit entities have much narrower eligibility unless Indigenous, part of a consortium, and/or benefits are broadly distributed.

## Mandatory application readiness

The current intake requires:

- completed online application;
- detailed project cost sheet;
- externally reviewed financial statements for at least two complete years;
- listing of all non-PacifiCan project funding;
- evidence for confirmed non-PacifiCan funding;
- non-PacifiCan funding of at least 50% of total project cost.

## Assessment factors

PacifiCan says applications are assessed on areas including:

- eligibility;
- alignment with PacifiCan priorities;
- measurable economic benefits;
- strong market/industry demand;
- governance and established management capacity;
- financial capacity;
- private investment relative to government assistance.

## Afghan Hub — current fit assessment

### Potential strengths

- digital platform with professional/business-networking capability;
- organizations/business directory;
- opportunity discovery;
- potential future support for entrepreneurs and small businesses;
- multilingual accessibility;
- AI-enabled discovery architecture.

### Current gaps

1. **Business-ecosystem purpose is not yet the primary Afghan Hub mandate.**  
   Afghan Hub currently has a broader community/professional-network function.

2. **Two years of externally reviewed financial statements may be unavailable for the relevant applicant entity.**

3. **50% matching funding may not be secured.**

4. **Economic-impact evidence is not yet mature.**  
   RIE wants measurable business/SME outcomes, not only community engagement.

5. **Governance/delivery scale may be insufficient for a large RIE project unless an established partner or consortium is involved.**

## Go / no-go gate

Do **not** prepare a full RIE application unless all are true:

- [ ] eligible legal applicant entity confirmed;
- [ ] at least two complete years of externally reviewed financial statements available;
- [ ] at least 50% non-PacifiCan project funding can be evidenced;
- [ ] project directly supports B.C. businesses/innovators/entrepreneurs;
- [ ] measurable economic outcomes can be credibly projected;
- [ ] project management/governance capacity can support delivery;
- [ ] strong business/industry demand can be evidenced;
- [ ] project budget is large enough to justify the application burden.

## Stronger RIE framing if pursued later

A future version of Afghan Hub could become a better RIE fit through a dedicated economic-development workstream such as:

**Afghan Hub Business & Innovation Network**

Potential functions:
- entrepreneur/business discovery;
- procurement opportunity navigation;
- mentorship;
- supplier/business matching;
- founder peer networks;
- digital adoption support;
- business events and workshops;
- professional talent matching.

The AI layer could then support:
- business-to-resource matching;
- opportunity discovery;
- mentor/supplier discovery;
- ecosystem navigation.

But these features should be built because they serve a real economic-development need, not only to fit a grant.

## Recommended 2026 decision

### Current recommendation: **No-go for a rushed standalone application unless an eligible established partner emerges.**

The October 30 intake is strategically interesting but likely too heavy if Afghan Hub cannot immediately demonstrate:
- two years of reviewed financials;
- 50% confirmed matching funds;
- direct B.C. SME/business outcomes;
- established governance capacity.

### Better use of time

1. Build product/pilot evidence.
2. Develop business/entrepreneur support use cases.
3. Collect organization/business demand evidence.
4. Explore established ecosystem partners.
5. Monitor the next RIE intake; PacifiCan has stated this intake model is intended to open twice per year.
