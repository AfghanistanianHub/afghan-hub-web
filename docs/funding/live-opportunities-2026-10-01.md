# Afghan Hub Funding Watchlist — 2026-10-01

This file records live opportunities verified on 2026-10-01. Re-check official sources before applying because program status and eligibility can change.

## 1. B.C. Inclusive Communities Grants (BC-ICG)

**Status:** Open  
**Deadline:** October 19, 2026  
**Maximum grant:** $5,000  
**Applicant requirement:** B.C.-based non-profit organization  
**Project timing:** Projects must start on or after April 1, 2027 and finish by March 31, 2028.

Program focus includes inclusion, participation, multicultural understanding, and reducing racism/discrimination.

### Afghan Hub fit

Potentially useful for a tightly scoped community-inclusion component rather than the full AI build.

Possible pilot framing:

**Afghan Hub Multilingual Community Navigation Pilot**

A small, community-led pilot focused on making local community information and participation easier to discover across language and network barriers.

### Gate before applying

Confirm Afghan Hub has, or can apply through, an eligible B.C.-based non-profit legal entity. Do not assume eligibility from the product alone.

Official source:
https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026AG0064-001064

## 2. PacifiCan — Regional Innovation Ecosystems (RIE)

**Status:** Open  
**Deadline:** October 30, 2026 at 12:00 p.m. PT  
**Funding type:** Non-repayable contributions for eligible ecosystem projects.

The intake is designed primarily for organizations that support businesses, innovators and entrepreneurs in British Columbia.

The current application requires, among other items:
- externally reviewed financial statements for at least two complete years;
- a detailed project cost sheet;
- evidence/listing of non-PacifiCan funding;
- non-PacifiCan funding of at least 50% of project costs.

### Afghan Hub fit

Strategically interesting if Afghan Hub develops a credible business/entrepreneur support component or applies through an eligible consortium/organization.

It is **not** a clean immediate fit for a small early-stage community platform merely because the project uses AI.

### Gate before applying

Confirm:
- eligible legal entity;
- two years of required financial statements;
- ability to show at least 50% non-PacifiCan project funding;
- direct benefits to B.C. businesses/innovators/entrepreneurs;
- governance and delivery capacity.

Official sources:
https://www.canada.ca/en/pacific-economic-development/services/funding/regional-innovation-ecosystems/rie-how-to-apply.html
https://www.canada.ca/en/pacific-economic-development/services/funding/regional-innovation-ecosystems/rie-who-can-apply.html

## 3. PacifiCan — Regional Artificial Intelligence Initiative (RAII)

**Status:** Not currently accepting applications.

PacifiCan states that additional funding has been announced and that more application information will be provided. The current program information lists not-for-profit projects at $500,000 to $5 million and emphasizes responsible AI, data security/governance, measurable economic benefits and organizational capacity.

### Afghan Hub fit

This is a longer-horizon target, not an immediate application.

Afghan Hub would need substantially stronger scale, organizational capacity, economic-impact evidence and likely partnership structure before a large RAII application is competitive.

### Preparation now

Collect:
- AI adoption metrics;
- multilingual user outcomes;
- privacy/data-governance documentation;
- responsible-AI evaluation;
- partner letters;
- economic/community impact evidence;
- delivery capacity and co-funding plan.

Official source:
https://www.canada.ca/en/pacific-economic-development/services/funding/regional-artificial-intelligence-initiative.html

## 4. IRCC Settlement Program funding

**Status:** No general open Afghan-Hub-specific intake confirmed in this review.

IRCC funds settlement services including needs assessment/referrals, information/orientation, employment-related support and integration. This makes Afghan Hub's navigation concept strategically relevant to future calls, but eligibility and target populations must be matched to the exact funding process.

### Afghan Hub fit

Potentially strong for future solicitations if the project is framed around:
- information and referral;
- newcomer navigation;
- employment/service discovery;
- multilingual access;
- measurable settlement outcomes.

Do not describe the broader Afghan Hub user base as equivalent to an IRCC-eligible settlement-client population. Any future application must separate the general platform from the funded eligible-user/service scope.

Official source:
https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/funding.html

## 5. Vancouver Foundation

The 2026 grant calendar includes multiple programs for B.C. non-profits/charities. Fit depends heavily on the specific program.

A currently open Participatory Action Research intake closes October 13, 2026, but it is health-related and requires collaboration between a community service organization and an institution with access to an ethics board. It is therefore not an obvious fit for the current Afghan Hub AI product scope.

### Afghan Hub approach

Monitor Vancouver Foundation for community/inclusion/system-change programs that match Afghan Hub's legal structure and project outcomes. Do not force-fit the AI feature into a grant whose program objective is unrelated.

Official source:
https://www.vancouverfoundation.ca/grant-seekers/find-grants/
