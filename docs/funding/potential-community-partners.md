## Outreach ask

Keep the first outreach lightweight:

1. introduce Afghan Hub in 2–3 sentences;
2. explain the Multilingual Community Navigator pilot;
3. ask for a 20–30 minute feedback conversation;
4. ask whether they would consider:
   - reviewing terminology,
   - referring pilot participants,
   - validating community-resource discovery,
   - or providing a short support letter if they find the project useful.

Do not ask an organization to act as the grant applicant until its eligibility, capacity and willingness are confirmed.
