**Maximum grant:** $5,000

> **Eligibility gate:** The 2026-27 program requires the applicant to be a registered B.C.-based non-profit organization in good standing, with a B.C. address, serving eligible racialized/Indigenous/faith communities, operational at application time, and with an annual budget under $1 million. ## Working project title

**Afghan Hub Multilingual Community Navigation Pilot**

## Funding category fit

Primary fit: **Intercultural Understanding and Exchange / Community Resilience Building**

The project should be presented as a community inclusion and participation pilot. AI is an enabling technology, not the grant's purpose.

## Project summary

Afghan community members in British Columbia often discover local events, organizations, volunteer roles, professional connections and community resources through fragmented websites, social media pages and informal networks. This can make participation harder for people who are unfamiliar with local systems, have limited professional networks or prefer Dari or Pashto.

The Afghan Hub Multilingual Community Navigation Pilot will test a simple digital navigation experience that helps community members discover relevant organizations, events, opportunities and people in a more accessible way. The pilot will include multilingual community testing, guided discovery prompts, community feedback sessions and an evaluation of whether the tool makes it easier for participants to connect with community activities and organizations.

The pilot will not provide legal or immigration advice and will not make automated eligibility decisions.

## Community need

The project addresses three practical barriers:

1. **Fragmented information** — relevant organizations, events and opportunities are distributed across many channels.
2. **Language and terminology barriers** — users may know what they need without knowing the institutional or category language used by service directories.
3. **Network-access barriers** — people with smaller local networks may be less likely to hear about opportunities through word of mouth.

## Objectives

1. Improve access to community information for Afghan British Columbians.
2. Increase discovery of multicultural and community participation opportunities.
3. Test English, Dari and Pashto navigation with community members.
4. Gather evidence on which language, terminology and discovery patterns create barriers.
5. Strengthen connections between community members and participating organizations.

## Proposed activities

### 1. Multilingual discovery design
Prepare English, Dari and Pashto prompts and interface text for common community goals such as:
- finding local organizations;
- discovering events;
- finding volunteer opportunities;
- finding professional/community connections.

### 2. Community testing sessions
Run structured usability/feedback sessions with community participants representing different ages, language preferences and levels of familiarity with local services.

### 3. Community organization validation
Invite local organizations to review how their public information is represented and identify terminology that makes discovery easier or harder.

### 4. Pilot launch
Release the read-only navigation experience to a limited pilot group.

### 5. Evaluation
Measure:
- whether participants find a relevant destination;
- result click-through;
- time/steps required to discover a resource;
- language used;
- participant-reported ease of use;
- suggestions for improvement.

## Expected outputs

- multilingual English/Dari/Pashto navigation prompts;
- community-tested Afghan Hub navigation pilot;
- at least 2 community feedback sessions;
- a small partner/organization review group;
- pilot usage and accessibility findings;
- final community-learning summary.

## Expected outcomes

Short-term:
- participants find community information more easily;
- increased awareness of organizations, events and participation opportunities;
- better understanding of multilingual digital-access barriers.

Medium-term:
- stronger community participation;
- improved connections between community members and organizations;
- evidence to improve inclusive digital community infrastructure.

## Responsible technology

The pilot is intentionally limited:
- read-only discovery;
- no automated legal/immigration decisions;
- no autonomous messaging or applications;
- no sale of user data;
- privacy-aware data access;
- links back to underlying community information;
- multilingual evaluation before broader rollout.

## Why Afghan Hub

Afghan Hub is being developed as a community and professional discovery platform connecting people, organizations, businesses, opportunities and events. The proposed pilot uses that existing foundation to test a more accessible navigation method rather than creating a disconnected standalone tool.
