# Afghan Hub — Innovate BC November 2026 Funding Watch

**Verified:** 2026-10-01

## 1. Commercialization and Investment Readiness Microgrant

**Status:** Opens November 2, 2026  
**Deadline:** November 27, 2026  
**Maximum grant:** $30,000  
**Coverage:** Up to 75% of eligible project costs  
**Applicant cash contribution:** Minimum 25%  
**Applicant type:** For-profit company headquartered in British Columbia, incorporated and in good standing.

### Why this may matter for Afghan Hub

This program does **not** fund software engineering, product development or routine operating costs.

Potentially relevant Afghan Hub use cases, if an eligible for-profit entity owns/commercializes the platform, include:

- privacy and cybersecurity readiness;
- governance systems;
- procurement readiness;
- legal/IP/commercial readiness;
- enterprise/customer due-diligence preparation;
- CRM/revenue-operations infrastructure where tied to a defined commercialization barrier;
- professional planning for future research or partnership arrangements.

### Stronger possible project framing

**Afghan Hub Commercial and Trust Readiness Project**

A defined professional-services project to prepare Afghan Hub for institutional partnerships, procurement, privacy/security due diligence and sustainable commercialization.

Possible outputs:
- privacy/data-governance package;
- cybersecurity assessment and remediation plan;
- procurement/vendor readiness package;
- IP/commercialization strategy;
- enterprise partnership framework;
- investment/customer pitch and diligence materials.

### Eligibility gate

Proceed only if:
- Afghan Hub is operated/commercialized through an eligible B.C.-headquartered for-profit company;
- the company owns/licenses the required IP;
- the project addresses a specific commercial/investment-readiness barrier;
- at least 25% cash contribution is available;
- an arm's-length professional service provider can provide a quote/scope of work;
- funded activities can be completed by March 2027.

### Important exclusions

Do not propose:
- AI/software feature development;
- ordinary coding;
- general staff salaries;
- advertising/lead generation;
- routine SaaS/hosting;
- equipment/infrastructure;
- open-ended consulting retainers.

### Required application materials

The official program page states applicants will need:
- company background;
- market opportunity;
- project description;
- budget and timeline;
- quote/scope from qualified service provider;
- current customer/investor pitch deck;
- brief IP strategy;
- risk management matrix.

Official source:
https://www.innovatebc.ca/commercialization-and-investment-readiness-microgrant-innovate-bc

## 2. Integrated Marketplace — Early-Stage Demonstration Call

**Status:** Open  
**Deadline:** October 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. PT  
**Maximum contribution:** Up to 40% of eligible project costs, maximum $500,000.

### Eligibility requirements include

- B.C.-based SME;
- legally incorporated and in good standing;
- technology at TRL 6 or higher;
- real-world pilot/demonstration;
- committed B.C. early adopter/end user/pilot partner;
- measurable validation/customer-adoption/market-readiness outcomes;
- matching contributions;
- strategic-sector alignment.

### Afghan Hub fit

**Low / conditional fit at the current stage.**

A community-platform pilot alone should not be forced into this program. The call is aimed at technology demonstration under real operational conditions and strategic-sector outcomes.

Consider only if Afghan Hub later has:
- an eligible for-profit operating company;
- a specific technology product at TRL 6+;
- a committed institutional early adopter;
- a real-world deployment with measurable operational outcomes;
- sufficient matching funding.

Official source:
https://www.innovatebc.ca/integrated-marketplace-early-stage-demonstration-call

## Funding-path separation

Afghan Hub should keep two funding narratives distinct.

### Community/public-benefit track
Best suited to:
- B.C. Inclusive Communities;
- future settlement/inclusion calls;
- foundations;
- non-profit/community partnerships.

Pays for outcomes such as:
- community inclusion;
- multilingual access;
- participation;
- community testing;
- resource navigation.

### Commercial/innovation track
Potentially suited to Innovate BC/PacifiCan where legal eligibility exists.

Pays for outcomes such as:
- commercialization readiness;
- privacy/cybersecurity diligence;
- procurement readiness;
- real-world technology validation;
- business/innovation ecosystem impact.

Do not mix legal entities or cost claims across programs without confirming each funder's rules.
