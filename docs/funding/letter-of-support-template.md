On behalf of **[Organization Name]**, I am pleased to express our support for the **Afghan Hub Multilingual Community Navigation Pilot**.

Our organization works with **[brief description of community/population/services]** in **[location]**. Through this work, we recognize that community members can face practical barriers when trying to find relevant organizations, activities, volunteer opportunities, professional connections and other community information. Information is often distributed across multiple websites, social channels and informal networks, and terminology or language can make navigation more difficult.

Afghan Hub's proposed pilot aims to test a multilingual digital navigation experience that helps users discover relevant community information through ordinary-language requests in English, Dari and Pashto. The pilot is designed as a read-only discovery tool and will link users to underlying community information rather than making automated legal, immigration or eligibility decisions.

We believe the pilot has potential to contribute to:

- improved access to community information;
- increased awareness of organizations, events and participation opportunities;
- stronger community connections;
- improved multilingual digital accessibility; and
- useful evidence about barriers to community information discovery.
