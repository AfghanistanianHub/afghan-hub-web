## Evidence package for a funder

A strong future application should be able to attach or summarize:

1. screenshots of the Assistant and contextual prompts;
2. architecture diagram showing authenticated read-only retrieval;
3. English/Dari/Pashto evaluation results;
4. pilot KPI dashboard or aggregate report;
5. 2–5 community partner support statements;
6. concise privacy/responsible-AI summary;
7. pilot participant feedback;
8. implementation timeline and budget;
9. examples of improvements made from community feedback;
10. sustainability / operating-cost note.

## Minimum credible claims before pilot data

Safe to say now:

- Afghan Hub has implemented a multilingual read-only discovery Assistant.
- The Assistant searches structured Afghan Hub records.
- English, Dari and Pashto discovery flows are being tested.
- The pilot excludes autonomous writes and high-stakes decision-making.
- Privacy-minimal KPI instrumentation has been implemented.

Do **not** claim yet:

- improved user outcomes;
- reduced search time;
- increased participation;
- a specific success percentage;
- strong Dari/Pashto semantic understanding;
- community endorsement.

Those require pilot evidence.

## Pilot evidence targets

Suggested targets to measure, not promises:

- result-producing search rate;
- result click-through rate;
- zero-result rate;
- repeat Assistant use;
- multilingual usage mix;
- task completion in structured usability sessions;
- partner-reported terminology improvements;
- number of community destinations discovered;
- safety/privacy incident count.

## Reporting discipline

Every future grant report should distinguish:

- **implemented feature**
- **observed metric**
- **participant-reported outcome**
- **project hypothesis**

This avoids turning product assumptions into unsupported impact claims.
