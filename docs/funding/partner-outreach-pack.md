# Afghan Hub — First-Wave Partner Outreach Pack

**Prepared:** 2026-10-01

This pack is for initial relationship-building around the Multilingual Community Navigation Pilot. It is not a grant-applicant request.

## Core outreach objective

Secure 20–30 minute conversations with community organizations to:

- validate the problem;
- test English/Dari/Pashto terminology;
- identify real community-navigation use cases;
- recruit pilot feedback participants;
- determine whether a short letter of support would be appropriate.

## General outreach message

**Subject:** Afghan Hub community navigation pilot — quick feedback conversation

Hello [Name/Organization],

I’m working on Afghan Hub, a community and professional discovery platform designed to make it easier to find people, organizations, opportunities and events connected to the Afghan community.

We’re now testing a small multilingual Community Navigator that lets users describe what they are looking for in ordinary language, including English, Dari and Pashto, and then directs them to relevant Afghan Hub information. The first pilot is read-only and is focused on discovery and accessibility rather than automated actions or advice.

Because of [Organization]'s work with [community/program area], I would really value your perspective before we take the pilot further. I’m especially interested in whether the terminology and discovery paths reflect the questions people actually ask, and where existing information is hardest to find.

Would someone from your organization be open to a short 20–30 minute feedback conversation?

There is no commitment attached to the conversation. If the project seems useful after you review it, we may also ask whether you would be comfortable helping with pilot feedback or a short letter of support for an aligned community grant.

Thank you,
Afghan Hub
https://app.apnbc.ca/

## Conversation agenda

Keep the first meeting practical:

1. What information do community members most often struggle to find?
2. What words do they naturally use in English/Dari/Pashto?
3. What should Afghan Hub never automate?
4. What makes a result/resource feel trustworthy?
5. Would their organization participate in lightweight usability feedback?
6. Would they consider a support letter after seeing a working pilot?

## Follow-up evidence to record

After each conversation, record only organizational/project feedback needed for product/funding work:

- organization;
- date;
- participant role/title;
- top navigation problems;
- terminology feedback;
- potential pilot role;
- support-letter interest: yes / maybe / no;
- follow-up action.

Do not put private client stories or sensitive personal information into the project tracker.
