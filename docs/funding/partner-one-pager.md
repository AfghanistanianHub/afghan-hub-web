# Afghan Hub AI Community Navigator — One-Page Partner Brief

## What Afghan Hub is building

Afghan Hub is developing a multilingual community and professional discovery platform that helps people find relevant members, organizations, businesses, opportunities and events.

The next phase introduces the **Afghan Hub AI Community Navigator**: a natural-language discovery layer designed to reduce information, language and network-access barriers.

Instead of requiring users to understand platform categories or service terminology, the Navigator lets them describe what they need in ordinary language and receive grounded Afghan Hub results.

## Example user needs

- "Find volunteer opportunities in Vancouver."
- "Show me upcoming community events."
- "Find organizations that support employment."
- "Find professionals working in technology."
- equivalent discovery requests in Dari and Pashto.

## Pilot scope

The first pilot is intentionally read-only.

It will:
- search Afghan Hub member, organization, business, opportunity and event records;
- support English, Dari and Pashto discovery;
- link users directly to the underlying Afghan Hub result;
- test usability with community participants;
- measure whether users reach relevant resources more efficiently.

It will not:
- provide legal or immigration eligibility decisions;
- submit applications;
- send messages or connection requests automatically;
- modify user data without explicit user action.

## Why this matters

Community information is often fragmented across websites, social channels and informal networks. People may know the outcome they need without knowing the right organization name, category or institutional vocabulary.

The project is intended to improve:
- community participation;
- opportunity and service discovery;
- volunteer and event participation;
- professional networking;
- multilingual digital inclusion.

## Responsible AI approach

The pilot uses a grounded retrieval model:
- Afghan Hub records remain the source of truth;
- privacy-aware authenticated access;
- minimum necessary data;
- no autonomous high-stakes decisions;
- no autonomous data writes in the pilot;
- measurable safety and usefulness outcomes.

## What Afghan Hub is looking for from partners

Potential partners can help through one or more of the following:

- community feedback and user testing;
- review of terminology and multilingual accessibility;
- validation of public organization/resource information;
- referral of interested pilot participants;
- a short letter of support for aligned grant applications;
- future co-design or funding partnerships.

## Pilot outcomes to measure

- percentage of searches producing a useful result;
- result click-through rate;
- member/organization/opportunity/event discovery;
- successful English/Dari/Pashto discovery;
- user-reported ease of use;
- zero unauthorized write actions;
- zero confirmed private-field disclosures.

## Current stage

- core Afghan Hub platform exists;
- read-only Assistant retrieval API is under implementation;
- English/Dari/Pashto intent support is being added;
- native Assistant UI is being integrated;
- funding and community-testing packages are being prepared.
