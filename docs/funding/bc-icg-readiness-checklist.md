# BC Inclusive Communities Grant — Afghan Hub Readiness Checklist

**Verified:** 2026-10-01  
**Deadline:** October 19, 2026  
**Maximum grant:** $5,000  
**Project window:** Start on or after April 1, 2027; finish by March 31, 2028.

## 1. Applicant eligibility gate

Before investing time in the application, confirm the applicant organization is:

- a registered B.C.-based non-profit organization;
- in good standing;
- using a B.C. address;
- operational at the time of application;
- serving racialized, Indigenous and/or faith communities that experience racialization;
- operating with an annual budget under $1 million.

If Afghan Hub itself does not meet these requirements, identify an eligible non-profit partner only if the partnership is genuine and the partner is willing to be the accountable applicant.

## 2. Best-fit stream for Afghan Hub

For a Metro Vancouver-based pilot, the strongest possible fit appears to be an **Urban Community Grant**.

Potential categories to test against the final guidelines:

### Intercultural Understanding and Exchange
Good fit if the project emphasizes:
- cross-cultural participation;
- community dialogue;
- multilingual community access;
- storytelling or cultural exchange components.

### Community Resilience Building
Good fit if the project emphasizes:
- participation by under-represented groups;
- community connection;
- reducing isolation or information barriers;
- local community planning/engagement.

Avoid framing the application as "fund our AI product." The grant purpose should remain inclusion, participation and community connection; technology is the delivery mechanism.

## 3. Eligible-cost discipline

Examples the Province identifies as appropriate include:

- equipment rental;
- event/workshop materials;
- stationery;
- project coordinator fees;
- workshop fees.

The program states it will not fund expenses such as:

- salaries/fees not directly related to project delivery;
- capital projects;
- major equipment purchases;
- alcohol;
- recurring business expenses.

### Afghan Hub budget implication

Prefer:
- multilingual review/translation tied directly to the pilot;
- project coordination specifically for the funded project;
- participant/community workshop costs;
- project-specific accessibility/UX work;
- evaluation/reporting directly tied to the pilot.

Do not use the micro-grant to pay ordinary hosting, recurring SaaS, unrelated general salaries or equipment purchases.

- short evaluation plan;
- organization/partner support where useful.

## 6. Remaining application blockers

### Legal applicant
**Unconfirmed.** Do not submit until the eligible B.C. non-profit applicant is identified and verified.

### Final budget eligibility
Review every line against the final application guide/form before submission.

### Community partner evidence
Aim to secure at least 1–3 meaningful community support statements or letters if the application permits them.

### Pilot timeline
Use a funded project start date no earlier than April 1, 2027.

## 7. Suggested micro-pilot outcomes

By project end:

- multilingual discovery experience tested in English, Dari and Pashto;
- at least two structured community feedback sessions;
- community organizations/resources reviewed for discoverability;
- pilot participants able to find relevant community destinations more easily;
- documented lessons on language and information-access barriers;
- final short community-learning report.

## 8. Go / no-go decision

Proceed with the application only if all are true:

- [ ] eligible B.C. non-profit applicant confirmed;
- [ ] applicant annual budget is under $1M;
- [ ] project purpose clearly aligns with inclusion/multicultural participation;
- [ ] expenses are project-specific and eligible;
- [ ] project begins April 1, 2027 or later;
- [ ] accountable project lead is identified;
- [ ] budget totals no more than $5,000;
- [ ] application can be completed before October 19, 2026.
